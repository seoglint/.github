# SEOGlint

**Analyze. Optimize. Grow.**

SEOGlint provides free tools and educational resources for technical SEO and AI-search readiness.

We help developers and website owners understand metadata, JSON-LD structured data, XML sitemaps, robots.txt, canonical URLs, and other important optimization signals.

Explore our free tools at [seoglint.com](https://www.seoglint.com/).

## What we publish here

| Repository | What it contains |
|---|---|
| [seoglint-seo-resources](https://github.com/seoglint/seoglint-seo-resources) | Technical SEO templates: robots.txt (including AI crawlers), JSON-LD, XML sitemaps, meta tags, llms.txt, and an audit checklist |

More repositories will be added only when they are useful on their own.

## Topics we cover

- Technical SEO: indexing, canonicals, sitemaps, robots.txt
- Structured data: JSON-LD and schema.org
- AI-search readiness: search crawlers vs. AI training crawlers vs. user-triggered fetchers
- llms.txt: what it is, and what it does not do
